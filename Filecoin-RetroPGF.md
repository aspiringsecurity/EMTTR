# 🩺 OP Medicine – Personalized & Affordable Healthcare on Filecoin

## 🎯 Mission
Empowering developers to build **affordable, accessible, and personalized medicine applications** through **Web3 healthcare infrastructure** — powered by **Filecoin, Ethereum, and Optimism**.

---

## 🧬 Overview

**OP Medicine** introduces **EMTTR – Electronic Medicine Trial and Test Records as a Service**, a decentralized system for **secure, transparent, and verifiable clinical trial management**.

It provides developer tools for **medical data pipelines**, enabling open innovation in healthcare through Filecoin-based decentralized storage and programmable smart contracts.

Our goal is to make **personalized medicine affordable and accessible** by offering open-source developer tools and APIs to securely store, verify, and share medical trial data, health records, and radiology assets.

---

## ⚙️ Core Components

- **EMTTR Developer Tools:** APIs and smart contracts for electronic medicine trial and test records.  
- **Decentralized EHRs & Radiology Platforms:** Filecoin-powered medical data storage, retrieval, and audit trails.  
- **Web3 Integration Stack:** Built with **Lightlink**, **Blockscout**, and **Liteflow** NFT marketplace tools for traceable medical research data.  
- **PPT Token (Prescription & Payment Token):**  
  ERC-20 token enabling **token-gated access** for developers and healthcare providers.

## 🧠 Why Filecoin RetroPGF Matters

Filecoin’s programmable storage and verification capabilities enable **verifiable medical data pipelines** that lower the cost of storing, testing, and accessing sensitive healthcare data.

**OP Medicine** extends Filecoin’s ecosystem to **public health and civic technology**, creating open-source tools that empower developers, researchers, and healthcare innovators to build **trustless, affordable, and data-sovereign solutions**.
